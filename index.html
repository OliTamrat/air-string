Air Strings

Draw strings in the air and play them with your hands. Pinch and drag to draw a string, flick across it to pluck, pinch the middle and pull to bend the note, pinch an end to resize it.

The camera picture is processed on this device and is never sent anywhere. On an iPhone, turn off silent mode to hear it.

Songbook

Pick a song. The strings retune to its notes, then you play along with the backing.

Your song

Type the melody as note names with an octave number. Add :beats when a note is not one beat long, and R for a rest. Bar lines ( | ) are optional. Up to 10 different notes.

Instrument

Tap one to hear it. The strings you draw take on its sound.
